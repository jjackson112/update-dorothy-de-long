Virtual Workshop

October 17, 2025

3:00 pm

Join me for an immersive online workshop where we explore how to create depth, mood, and movement using light. Whether you’re a beginner looking to build confidence or an experienced artist wanting to refine your technique, this session gives you practical tools you can apply immediately.


We’ll cover:


  • How to block in simple shapes to establish strong composition
  • Techniques for capturing warm and cool light
  • Brushwork approaches for soft transitions and dramatic highlights
  • Tips for building atmosphere with color layering

You’ll follow along by-step as I demonstrate the full process in real time. There will be plenty of room for questions, feedback, and experimenting with your own piece.


Format:

Live Zoom session

Materials:

Any medium you’re comfortable with (recommended: acrylics, oils, or digital tablet)

Who it's for:

Artists of all levels

Wht you'll leave with:

A finished piece, new techniques, and the confidence to tackle lighting in your own work
