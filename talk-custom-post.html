Artist Talk

June 6, 2025

1:00 pm

Join me for an intimate conversation about the creative process, the inspirations that shape my work, and the lessons learned along the way. I’ll walk through selected pieces from my recent collection, sharing the ideas, challenges, and breakthroughs that brought each artwork to life.


We'll cover


  • How a concept evolves from sketch to finished piece
  • The real-world experiences and emotions that influence my themes
  • Technical decisions behind color, composition, and texture
  • What I’ve learned from failing, experimenting, and pushing boundaries

This session is designed to give you a deeper look into the art-making journey and open up space for questions and discussion. Whether you’re an artist, collector, or simply curious about the creative process, you’ll walk away with fresh insight and a closer connection to the work.


Format:

Live talk with Q&A

Ideal for:

Art lovers, students, emerging creators, or anyone interested in visual storytelling

What you'll gain:

Inspiration, practical takeaways, and a behind-the-scenes view of an artist’s world
